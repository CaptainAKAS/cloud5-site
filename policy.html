Order & Refund Policy

Orders & lead times

Everything at Cloud5 is made to order — nothing sits on a shelf. Please confirm your lead time with us on WhatsApp when you place your order.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the agreed delivery or pickup date.

Quality issues

If something isn't right, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo — we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Perishable goods

Our products are perishable, so we can't accept returns. Refrigerate cream-based desserts and consume everything within the advised window.

Allergens

Our kitchen handles gluten, eggs, dairy, tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, and soy — ask us about any allergy before ordering.
