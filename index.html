Our Story

Meet Jasmin, the heart of Cloud5

Hello — I'm Jasmin, the creator of Cloud5. I've been baking for over 25 years, and it's always been my dream to open my own business. After years of encouragement, I finally decided to open Cloud5.

I began with just a few cakes and bites — and now I'm adding something new to the menu almost every week. I have so many ideas, and I can't wait to share them with you.

We use real ingredients, and everything is made to order — nothing sits on a shelf. Thank you for supporting my journey, and I hope every bite feels like you're on Cloud5.
